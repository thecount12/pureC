Printed POSIX name     Plan 9 after you swap common.h
-------------------    --------------------------------
printf(...)            print(...)
NULL                   nil
exit(n)                exits(nil) or exits("error")
FILE *                 Biobuf *
fopen(path, "r")       Bopen(path, OREAD)
fopen(path, "w")       Bopen(path, OWRITE)
fgets / fprintf        Brdstr / Bprint
fclose(fp)             Bterm(bp)

The language in the listings is meant to compile on 8c once those
names change: declare variables at the start of a block, no
for (int i = 0).
